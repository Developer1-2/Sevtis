The Process

How the Partnership Works

From application to your first paid job — most partners are operational within one week.

1

Submit Your Application

Fill in the partner application form below with your background, skills and area of operation. We review all applications within 48 hours.

Free — no cost to apply
2

Vetting & Interview

A Sevtis team member contacts you for a short phone or in-person interview to verify your credentials and discuss how we'll work together.

Typically 30–45 minutes
3

Product Training

Attend a one or two-day hands-on training session covering the systems you'll install: CCTV, alarm panels, access control and/or biometric readers. Training is held at our Gulu office or on-site.

Certificate awarded on completion
4

Sign the Partner Agreement

A simple, transparent agreement covering referral rates, quality standards, use of the Sevtis brand and the terms of our working relationship.

5

Start Earning

You're added to our active partner network. Start receiving client referrals, purchasing equipment at wholesale rates, and earning on every job you complete.

First job typically within 2 weeks

Requirements

What We Look For
in a Partner

We maintain high standards so every client gets an excellent experience — regardless of which partner delivers their installation.

Technical Requirements

Skills and qualifications

Relevant Technical Qualification Required

Certificate, diploma or degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Telecommunications, or equivalent field.

Minimum 1 Year Field Experience Required

Demonstrated hands-on experience in electrical, low-voltage or IT systems installation.

Prior CCTV or Alarm Experience Preferred

Previous work with Hikvision, Dahua, Axis or similar security platforms is a strong advantage but not mandatory.

Network / IP Knowledge Preferred

Basic understanding of IP networking (subnets, PoE, DHCP) is very helpful for modern IP-camera setups.

Own Hand & Power Tools Required

Basic installation tools: drill, cable stripper, crimper, multimeter. Specialised equipment can be borrowed from Sevtis.

Professional Requirements

Conduct and documentation

Valid National ID or Passport Required

Government-issued identification for background verification and partner registration.

ERA or ERB Registration Preferred

Electricians registered with Uganda's Electricity Regulatory Authority or Engineers Registration Board are prioritised for electrical-scope jobs.

Defined Service Area Required

Clearly specify the districts or towns where you can operate. We match you with nearby clients only — no forced travel.

Reliable Mobile Contact Required

Must be reachable by phone or WhatsApp within 4 hours. Clients expect prompt communication and so does our team.

Maintain Quality Standards Required

All installations must meet Sevtis workmanship standards. Partners are reviewed after every job — a minimum 4-star client rating is required to remain active.

Earning Potential

What You Can Earn

Partner earnings depend on the type and volume of work you take on. Most solo engineer partners earn between UGX 400,000 and UGX 2,500,000 per installation depending on system complexity.

Installation Fees

Agreed per-project rates for labour — typically 15–25% of total project value depending on system type and scale.

Referral Commission

Refer a client to Sevtis — even if you don't do the install — and earn 5% of the total project value as a finder's fee.

Maintenance Contracts

Take on recurring monthly maintenance agreements on systems you've installed for predictable, passive income throughout the year.

Wholesale Equipment

Buy CCTV equipment, cable and accessories at our partner wholesale price — then supply to your own private clients at market rate.
