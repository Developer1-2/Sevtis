Surveillance

CCTV & video surveillance

See what happens on your property — live and recorded — with cameras matched to indoor, outdoor, and long-range needs.

A good CCTV system is not about the highest number of cameras. It is about covering the right angles: entrances, parking, stock areas, and blind spots, with image quality strong enough to identify people and vehicles when it matters.

Sevtis supplies IP and commercial-grade cameras (including Hikvision-class hardware), recorders, and remote viewing setup so you can check your site from your phone. We size the system to your building and budget, then install and hand over clearly.

Access control

Biometric & access control

Control who enters — and when — with facial recognition, fingerprint, or card systems that fit busy doors and staff gates.

Keys get copied and shared. Access control gives you a clear record of entry and the ability to revoke access when staff leave or tenants change. Facial and fingerprint terminals are fast for high-traffic doors; cards remain a simple backup.

We install readers, locks, and controllers for single doors or multi-door sites, and can link attendance where you need it. The goal is reliable daily use — not a system your team fights with every morning.

Intrusion

Alarm systems

Detect forced entry early with door contacts, motion sensors, and sirens — plus alerts on your phone when something triggers.

Cameras show what happened. Alarms help you respond when it is happening. A well-planned system covers likely entry points first — main doors, windows, and corridors — then expands as needed.

Sevtis installs wired and wireless kits suited to homes and small businesses, with outdoor sirens where a visible deterrent helps. We explain arming, zones, and app alerts so the system is used every day, not only the week after install.
